Pour + OnePortal

Your forecast, in OnePortal, before your coffee gets cold

Pour is our Google Chrome extension that connects Predict directly to OnePortal. Instead of retyping hundreds of forecast values by hand, Pour reads your forecast, calibrates it against what's already in the portal, and fills everything in for you — accurately, every time.

  1. Generate your forecast in Predict Upload your sales history and let the model suite produce your predictions.
  2. Open OnePortal with the Pour extension installed Pour lives right in Chrome — no extra software, no IT tickets, no separate logins.
  3. Smart calibration checks your numbers Pour aligns your forecast with the items and periods in OnePortal so everything lands where it should.
  4. One click. Done. Pour writes the forecast into OnePortal automatically — saving hours of entry and eliminating typos.

Questions

The short answers

The things people ask us most. The full list lives on our FAQs page.

What is Predict & Pour?›
Predict generates forecast outputs using multiple forecasting models. Pour is a Chrome extension that helps apply those forecasts inside OnePortal quickly and consistently.
How does Pour know what product I'm viewing?›
The extension reads the current product's PDCN from the OnePortal page and finds the matching row in your uploaded file. Then it fills Week 0–12 fields automatically.
Does Predict & Pour store my data in the cloud?›
For the extension workflow, your uploaded forecast data is stored locally on your own computer in Chrome storage. Predict & Pour cannot see your data, forecasts, or files.
Do I need to install anything else?›
No. Pour runs entirely as a Chrome extension — no desktop software, no server setup, and no changes to OnePortal itself. If the POUR button doesn't appear, confirm you're on the OnePortal forecasting page and refresh.
